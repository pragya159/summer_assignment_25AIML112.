Beginner Crypto Primer

What is Hashrate (TH/s)?
Hashrate measures the computational speed of crypto mining hardware. Higher hashrate means more calculations per second, leading to greater daily mining rewards.
Why E-Mining over Home Mining?
Home mining requires expensive $3,000+ ASIC computers, loud cooling fans (75dB+), and high residential electricity bills. E-mining bundles green industrial power and enterprise maintenance for pure passive returns.
What is USDT?
USDT (Tether) is a stable digital currency pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar. It lets you transfer funds globally in seconds with near-zero gas fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. If you can't find what you are looking for, chat with our instant AI assistant or submit a ticket.

What is e-mining and how is it different from normal crypto trading? ▼
Unlike speculative day trading where you bet on price swings, e-mining is an infrastructure investment. You lease computing power in physical data centers that solve mathematical puzzles to mint new cryptocurrency daily. You earn rewards regardless of short-term market fluctuations.
How does the 2% platform fee work? ▼
We believe in total transparency. Our platform retains a strictly performance-based 2% fee exclusively on the profits your allocated rigs generate. We NEVER touch or deduct fees from your principal deposit. The 2% covers on-site technicians, green power grid connections, and 24/7 security.
Which payment methods are accepted and how fast are they verified? ▼
We accept USDT (both TRC-20 and ERC-20 networks) as well as credit/debit card (USD). Payments are verified automatically through our gateway API within 3 to 10 seconds of blockchain broadcast, instantly transitioning to "Credited" status.
When can I withdraw my profits and what is the minimum? ▼
There are zero lockup restrictions on your accrued profits. You can withdraw your earnings anytime as long as you have at least 10.00 USDT or USD in your balance. Withdrawals are broadcasted immediately to your external wallet.
How is my personal data and account secured? ▼
We implement industry standard PBKDF2 password hashing, HTTPS 256-bit TLS encryption, cold-storage asset escrow, and strict session token authorization. Your payment credentials are never stored on plaintext servers.
